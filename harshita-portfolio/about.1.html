About

The journey from imagination to reality.

An Architect turned Product & UX Designer.

Architecture taught me to notice how people move through spaces: where they pause, what feels natural, and what quietly creates friction. My Master's in HCI helped me bring that same lens into digital products.

Today, I design end-to-end experiences for complex workflows, AI-driven tools, and products that need to feel clear, usable, and trustworthy.
